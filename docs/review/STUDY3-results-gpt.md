# Study 3 results — pre-publication advisory review

No BLOCKER found. Three SHOULD-FIX findings and one NIT follow. No numerical discrepancy was found in the reviewed Study 3 claims; the concerns below concern wording and disclosure.

1. **SHOULD-FIX — “exactly” overstates the three-study headline.** `REPORT.md:9–10` says all six targets order the probes “exactly as” Agave's priority predicts. Across the three studies, this reads as perfect transaction-by-transaction agreement, whereas the report itself gives Agave's Study 1 effects as 0.90, 0.12 and 0.88 (`REPORT.md:99`) and describes streaming exceptions (`REPORT.md:30–33`). Study 3's 318/318 result is exact for its observed complete pairs, under its particular regimes; it does not make the earlier exceptions disappear. **Fix:** replace “exactly as” with “with label effects consistent with”, or state that reward ÷ (cost + 1) is identified among the registered candidates, followed by the existing discussion of strictness. Reserve the perfect-order statement for Study 3's complete pairs.

2. **SHOULD-FIX — make the boundary of “robust to selection” explicit.** `REPORT.md:444–457` and `README.md:17` correctly report the frozen selection verdict, but omit its important boundary beside that wording: the bound covers incomplete pairs **inside evaluable windows**, not excluded windows. PREREGISTRATION-3 §9 explicitly states that excluded windows lie outside the bound (`docs/PREREGISTRATION-3.md:182–184`). For BAM, the reported worst case uses 21 successes plus three incomplete pairs, hence 24 windows; its eight excluded windows are outside that calculation. The 30 exclusions are disclosed later in §6.5, but their relationship to the robustness claim is left implicit. **Fix:** add “within evaluable windows; the 30 `not_all_sent` windows are outside this bound” beside the robustness statement, and give the README a short equivalent qualifier or a precise link to it. Preserve the registered verdict and calculation.

3. **SHOULD-FIX — carry the temporal-stability assumption into the README.** `README.md:10` identifies each target's key, and `README.md:126` explains the registered-candidate/additive-key limitation, but neither explains that identification combines evidence from different days and assumes the key stayed unchanged. This assumption is explicit in frozen §10 (`docs/PREREGISTRATION-3.md:192–193`) and is correctly disclosed in `REPORT.md:583–584`. Without it, the README can read as though Study 3 alone eliminated CU price and fee ordering; those also predict both new contrasts (`docs/PREREGISTRATION-3.md:58–66`). **Fix:** append to the README's limitations bullet: “Identification combines the three studies and assumes each target's key was unchanged between their measurement dates.” Link that sentence to REPORT §9. No change to the frozen verdict is needed.

4. **NIT — finish the Study 3 failure accounting in §7.** `REPORT.md:525–526` lists 120 never-sent probes but omits the other non-landing outcome: 12 sent probes expired. The campaign log explicitly distinguishes 636 landed, 12 expired and 120 never sent (`docs/CAMPAIGN-LOG.md:354–358`); the ledger summary records 12 `expired_charged` entries totaling 93,000 lamports (`data/derived/ledgers/summary.json`, `study3.by_budget_and_status`). Their three lost BAM windows and conservative charge are already disclosed elsewhere (`REPORT.md:453–454,686–688`), so this is a local completeness issue, not an undisclosed cost. **Fix:** add that 648 probes were sent, 636 landed and 12 expired, corresponding to BAM's three wholly missing four-probe windows; distinguish the 93,000-lamport ledger charge from landed on-chain fees.
